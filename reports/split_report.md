## 5. split_group (sampling unit) and unmerged near-duplicate pairs

Records sharing a `dup_group` were merged into one representative by the clean step. Near-duplicate pairs the clean step listed but did not merge (same-source template series; undecided `dedup_review.csv` pairs; blocked links in `dedup_pairs.csv`) are joined (union-find) into one `split_group` when both records are on the same seen/unseen side, so two near-identical titles never land in two splits. By team default these records are kept, not deleted; joining only binds them to the same split. A seen–unseen pair cannot share a split: without a `separate` decision in `configs/checking/dedup_decisions.csv`, the seen record is held in reserve (`hold_reason=pending_dedup_review_with_unseen`) until the dedup review decides (`link` makes the clean step exclude the seen record as `duplicate_of_unseen`). Quotas count records; a group that does not fit the remaining quota of every (source, category) cell it touches is skipped and the next group in seed order is tried.

| Same-source template series (`template_series.csv`) | 521 | 521 | 521 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

## 7. Checks (all passed)

- Record counts match the quotas per source × split and the human subset
- Each eligible record appears exactly once in the manifest or the reserve
- Each split_group/dup_group belongs to one split (never divided between a split and the reserve)
- No train/dev/test_seen record shares a dup_group/split_group with an unseen record (seen copies of unseen records were excluded in the clean step)
- No unmerged near-duplicate pair (template series, `dedup_review.csv`, blocked links in `dedup_pairs.csv`; except pairs decided `separate`) has its records in two different splits
- Seen records with an unreviewed near-duplicate pair with an unseen record are in no split (held in reserve)
- The human subset lies inside train
- Every dev record has a sapo; the reserve has_sapo column matches the clean table
- Unseen sources only in test_unseen, seen sources never in test_unseen
- Blind tables and annotation_map cover exactly the human scope, annotation_id unique
- Deterministic: two builds from the same inputs give byte-identical outputs (otherwise the script stops without writing files)

## 8. Blind tables and mapping

- `data/annotations/blind/blind_dev.csv`: 1,400 rows (dev; annotated first, per the team flow).
- `data/annotations/blind/blind_train_subset_test.csv`: 8,600 rows (train subset + test_seen + test_unseen).
- Both tables hold only `annotation_id,title,category` (shared approved category); no source, URL, split or LLM label. Row order is a seeded permutation mixing source/category/split. Titles are kept as in clean; titles with internal line breaks: 1 (quoted in the CSV, read with a CSV reader).
- `annotation_id` = `A` + 5 digits sampled (seeded) from 10000–99999, not sequential by source, split or `record_id`.
- `data/manifests/annotation_map.csv` (internal, not sent to annotators): 10,000 rows `annotation_id, record_id, split, source`.
